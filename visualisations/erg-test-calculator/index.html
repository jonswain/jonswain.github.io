How pace fades with distance

Each line is a group of athletes with the same 2k speed, from the fastest tenth to the slowest. Near 2k, every group slows by a similar number of seconds per doubling of distance. The curve bends, though: pace changes much faster below about 1 km than above it, so a single fixed rule like Paul's Law (+5 s/500m per doubling) goes wrong for sprints. The bigger differences are between individuals of the same speed (next section).

Validation

How accurate is it?

One in five athletes was held out of the fit. For each held-out athlete, every ranked result was predicted from one or two of their other results and compared with the real time. Errors are in seconds per 500 m.
