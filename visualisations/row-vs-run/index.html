Row vs Run

Is a Concept2 erg time faster than a running time over the same distance? For almost everyone, yes. Running only wins at the very top: world-best runners leave the erg far behind, but below roughly the top 2–3% of men (and an even smaller slice of women) the erg is faster, and the weaker the athlete, the bigger the erg's lead.

The crossover

How good do you need to be before running wins?

Within each sport, finish times sit close to a normal curve. Fitting that curve to each sport's five standards, and finding where the two meet, gives the percentile at which a run and an erg piece take the same time. Above it running is faster; below it the erg is. This extrapolates beyond the Elite (95th percentile) tables, so treat it as an estimate.

Why the gap closes

The erg squashes everyone together

How close each standard gets to world-best speed, averaged over 1 km to 10 km. A beginner on the erg already moves at about two-thirds of world-best speed. A beginner runner moves at about 40%.

ErgRunning

The erg's pace scale compresses differences in fitness

The Concept2 monitor turns power into pace with a cube root: watts = 2.80 ÷ (seconds per metre)³. Eight times the power only doubles the speed. Running speed rises roughly in proportion to the energy you put in. So the same difference in fitness shows up about three times smaller on the erg. Body weight matters too: the erg supports your weight, while a runner carries every kilogram.
